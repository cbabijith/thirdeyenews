# Privacy Policy for ThirdEye News

**Last Updated:** July 9, 2026

ThirdEye News ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application, ThirdEye News (the "App"), including the Admin Portal.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the App.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Data
When you register, log in, or use the App (particularly as an admin or editor), we may collect:
- **Identity Data:** Full name, email address, and account passwords.
- **Role/Permissions Data:** User designation (e.g., admin, editor) to authenticate dashboard privileges.

### B. Device Permissions & Access
To provide media upload features, the App requests permission to access:
- **Camera:** To capture images directly for news articles or profile configurations.
- **Photos / Media / Files:** To pick images and graphics from your device's gallery for news stories or advertisement banners.
- **Internet Access:** To connect with our databases, retrieve the latest articles, and synchronize edits.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to:
- Create, manage, and secure your user account.
- Authenticate administrative access to the news dashboard, category portal, and advertisement settings.
- Upload and host media (images) attached to news reports and banner ads.
- Maintain and improve the performance of our news feed and database systems.

---

## 3. Data Storage and Sharing
- **Backend Services:** Our user accounts, article records, and media attachments are securely processed and hosted using **Supabase** (powered by PostgreSQL).
- **Third-Party Sharing:** We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third parties. Data is only shared with database hosting services to maintain App functionality.

---

## 4. Security of Your Information
We implement industry-standard security measures (such as secure HTTPS connection protocols and backend row-level security parameters) to maintain the safety of your personal information. However, no electronic transmission over the internet can be guaranteed 100% secure.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy
Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

---

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
